Mahatma Gandhi

Full Name Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi
Born 2 October 1869
Birthplace Porbandar, Gujarat
Died 30 January 1948

About Him

Mahatma Gandhi was one of the most influential leaders of India's freedom movement. He promoted truth, non-violence and civil disobedience as powerful methods of resistance against British rule.

Contribution to the Freedom Movement

  • Non-Cooperation Movement
  • Salt March and Dandi March
  • Quit India Movement
  • Promotion of Satyagraha and non-violence
  • Mobilization of ordinary Indians in the freedom struggle

Legacy

Gandhi's philosophy of non-violence influenced India's independence movement and inspired civil rights movements around the world.

Famous Thought

"Truth is God."
🧑🏻‍🎓

Bhagat Singh

Full Name Bhagat Singh
Born 28 September 1907
Birthplace Banga, Punjab region
Martyrdom 23 March 1931

About Him

Bhagat Singh was one of India's most famous revolutionaries. From a very young age, he dedicated his life to the cause of India's independence and became a powerful symbol of courage and sacrifice.

Major Contributions

  • Associated with the Hindustan Socialist Republican Association
  • Played an active role in revolutionary activities
  • Participated in the Central Legislative Assembly bombing
  • Fought for the rights of political prisoners
  • Inspired millions of young Indians

Legacy

Bhagat Singh remains an enduring symbol of bravery, patriotism and revolutionary spirit among Indian youth.

Famous Slogan

"Inquilab Zindabad!"

Subhas Chandra Bose

Popular Name Netaji
Born 23 January 1897
Birthplace Cuttack, Odisha
Major Organization Indian National Army

About Him

Subhas Chandra Bose was one of the major nationalist leaders of India's freedom movement. He believed that armed struggle could play an important role in achieving India's independence.

Indian National Army

Bose became the most prominent leader of the Indian National Army, also known as the Azad Hind Fauj. He sought to organize Indian forces to fight British rule and establish an independent India.

His Vision

Bose inspired Indians with his determination, leadership and strong commitment to complete independence.

Famous Slogan

"Give me blood, and I will give you freedom."
🧔🏽

Chandrashekhar Azad

Full Name Chandrashekhar Tiwari
Born 23 July 1906
Birthplace Bhabra, Madhya Pradesh
Martyrdom 27 February 1931

About Him

Chandrashekhar Azad was a fearless revolutionary who played an important role in India's revolutionary movement. He dedicated his life to fighting against British rule.

Major Contributions

  • Associated with the Hindustan Republican Association
  • Helped strengthen the revolutionary movement
  • Worked alongside several revolutionary leaders
  • Played an important role in organizing revolutionary activities
  • Inspired young Indians with his courage

Legacy

Azad became famous for his determination to remain free and his refusal to surrender to British authorities.

"I will face the enemy's bullets; I am free and will always remain free."
👩🏻

Rani Lakshmibai

Name Rani Lakshmibai
Born 19 November 1828
Birthplace Varanasi
Martyrdom 18 June 1858

About Her

Rani Lakshmibai was the Queen of Jhansi and became one of the most famous leaders associated with the uprising of 1857. She fought bravely against British forces.

Major Contributions

  • Defended the kingdom of Jhansi
  • Played an important role in the uprising of 1857
  • Resisted British expansion
  • Demonstrated extraordinary courage and leadership
  • Became a symbol of bravery for generations

Legacy

Rani Lakshmibai continues to be remembered as one of India's greatest symbols of courage, leadership and resistance.

"I shall not surrender my Jhansi."
👨🏻

Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel

Full Name Vallabhbhai Jhaverbhai Patel
Born 31 October 1875
Birthplace Nadiad, Gujarat
Popular Title Iron Man of India

About Him

Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel was one of the important leaders of India's freedom movement. After independence, he played a crucial role in bringing many princely states together into the Indian Union.

Major Contributions

  • Played an important role in the Kheda Satyagraha
  • Led the Bardoli Satyagraha
  • Made a significant contribution to the freedom movement
  • Played a major role in the integration of princely states
  • Served as India's first Deputy Prime Minister and Home Minister

Legacy

Patel is remembered as the "Iron Man of India" for his strong leadership and his important role in the political integration of independent India.

"Unity and integrity are among the greatest strengths of our nation."
